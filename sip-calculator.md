# Privacy Policy — SIP Calculator

**Effective Date:** 8 March 2025
**Last Updated:** 8 March 2025
**Platform:** Android (Google Play)

## Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how **SIP Calculator** ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use our Android application. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

> We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information entered into the App. All calculations happen entirely on your device.

---

## Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

- Investment amounts, tenures, or expected returns you enter
- Your name, email address, or phone number
- Your location or device identifiers
- Financial history or calculation history
- Any other personally identifiable information

All data you enter into the calculator is processed locally on your device and is discarded when you close the App.

---

## Advertising — Google AdMob

The App displays advertisements served by **Google AdMob**, a third-party advertising platform operated by Google LLC. To serve relevant ads, AdMob may automatically collect certain non-personal technical information from your device, including:

- Advertising ID (Android Ad ID) — used for ad personalisation
- IP address — used to infer approximate region for ad targeting
- Device type, OS version, and app version
- Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
- Crash and performance diagnostics

> This data is collected and processed by Google under their own Privacy Policy. We do not receive, access, or store this data ourselves.

You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time via your device settings:
**Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation**

Google's Privacy Policy: [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## Permissions

The App requests only the permissions necessary to display advertisements. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, storage, or location.
