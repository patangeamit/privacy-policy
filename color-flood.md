Privacy Policy – Color Flood

Last updated: March 2026

Color Flood (“the Game”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use the Game.

### 1. Information We Collect

The Game itself does not require account registration and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

However, certain information may be collected automatically by third-party services used in the Game, including advertising networks.

These services may collect information such as:

- Device type and operating system  
- Device identifiers  
- Approximate location information (such as country or region)  
- App usage information  
- Advertising identifiers used for ad delivery  

This information helps improve advertising relevance and maintain app performance.

### 2. Advertising

The Game displays advertisements provided by third-party ad networks. These ad providers may collect and process data to deliver personalized or non-personalized ads.

Examples of data that may be used by ad providers include:

- Advertising identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID)  
- Device information  
- Interaction with advertisements  

If you choose to purchase a “Remove Ads” option, advertisements may be disabled within the Game.

For more information about how advertising networks handle data, please refer to the privacy policies of those providers.

### 3. Local Data Storage

The Game stores certain gameplay information locally on your device, such as:

- Game progress  
- Unlocked levels  
- In-game currency  
- High scores  

This data is stored using local device storage and is not transmitted to external servers.

### 4. Data Security

The Game does not operate its own servers for storing user data. All gameplay information remains on your device unless shared by third-party services such as advertising networks.

### 5. Children’s Privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information through third-party services, please contact the relevant service provider.

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this policy.

### 7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer through the contact information provided on the app store listing.
