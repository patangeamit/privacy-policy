# Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 11, 2026

## Introduction
BMI Calculator ("the app") is designed to help users calculate their Body Mass Index (BMI) quickly and easily. This privacy policy explains how information is handled when using the app.

## Information We Collect
The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

All data entered into the app (such as height and weight) is used only for the BMI calculation and remains on the user's device.

## Data Storage
The app does not store personal data on external servers. All calculations are performed locally on the device.

## Third-Party Services
The app may use third-party services that could collect information used to identify you.

These services may include:

- Google AdMob (for displaying advertisements)
- Google Play Services

These third-party services have their own privacy policies regarding how they handle user data.

## Advertising
If the app displays advertisements, they may be provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers and other information to provide relevant ads.

You can learn more about how Google handles data here:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Children's Privacy
The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy
This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
